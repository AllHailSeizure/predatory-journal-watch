**Effective Date:** 2026-11-25

## 2. What the app does

The app checks links and DOIs in new posts and, optionally, in new comments. It compares them against public predatory or hijacked journal watchlists: the Retraction Watch Hijacked Journal Checker and Beall's List. When it finds a match, it posts an informational, locked reply. Each publisher gets at most one warning per post. The app never removes, reports, or hides content.

## 3. Nature of the warnings

A warning means that a domain or publisher **appears on a third-party list**. It is not a finding by the app's maintainers that any journal, publisher, author, or paper is fraudulent or low quality. Watchlists can be incomplete, out of date, or wrong. DOI resolution through doi.org, Crossref, and DataCite can fail or return stale data. Readers should judge sources for themselves.
